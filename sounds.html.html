How to use it in the pitch

  1. On your phone, open the Duet Jammer, choose Microphone, keep the jammer on Auto and press Start.
  2. On this device, set the same temperature, turn the volume up, and hold it 20 to 50 cm from the phone.
  3. Play Demo sequence. The jammer should show female, then male, then both, and start jamming when the duet begins.

These are computer-generated tones built from published values: female about 400 Hz and male about 600 Hz at 26 °C, matching at a shared harmonic near 1200 Hz (Cator et al., 2009), shifting about 10 Hz per °C. They are not recordings of real mosquitoes. Phone speakers are weak below 500 Hz, so play loudly.

Background: Aedes aegypti, female, dorsal view.
